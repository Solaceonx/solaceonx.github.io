N8e5 model overlays

Every available N8e5 CMC model snapshot older than 9 Gyr (all lineages, colored by WD-accretion efficiency) overlaid on each cluster's observed V-band surface-brightness profile. Models are rescaled to the cluster distance (r → r·Dref/D, Dref=10 kpc) and reddened by the cluster's AV. Observed points are Trager 1995 (KHW source removed).
